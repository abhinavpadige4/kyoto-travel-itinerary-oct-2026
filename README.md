# 🏯 Kyoto 5-Day Budget Itinerary - October 2026

**Planned for 2 People | Total Budget: Under $1,200 | Dates: October 10-14, 2026**

## Overview
This comprehensive travel plan details a 5-day trip to Kyoto, Japan focusing on temples, gardens, local food experiences, traditional tea houses, and a day trip to Nara - all while staying under $1,200 for two people.

## 🗓️ Daily Highlights

**Day 1: Arrival & Downtown Exploration**
- Nishiki Market food tour
- Pontocho Alley dinner
- Evening Kamogawa River walk

**Day 2: Eastern Kyoto Temples**
- Fushimi Inari Taisha at sunrise
- Kiyomizu-dera Temple
- Traditional Gion tea house experience

**Day 3: Western Kyoto Gardens**
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
- Tenryu-ji Temple Garden
- Iwatayama Monkey Park
- Matcha dessert in Arashiyama

**Day 4: Nara Day Trip**
- Todai-ji Temple (Great Buddha)
- Nara Park deer feeding
- Kasuga Taisha Shrine
- Naramachi historic district

**Day 5: Golden Pavilion & Departure**
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
- Philosopher's Path scenic walk
- Final souvenir shopping

## 🏨 Accommodation Recommendation
**Hotel Sunroute Plaza Kyoto** or similar business hotel
- Location: Near Kyoto Station
- Rate: ~¥8,000/night for double room
- Amenities: Clean, reliable, convenient transport access

## 🚇 Transportation Tips
- **Recommended:** ICOCA card for local transport + point-to-point tickets
- **Airport:** Haruka Express (¥2,850 each way)
- **Nara Trip:** JR Nara Line (~¥720 round trip without pass)
- **Local:** Buses and subways (¥800/day estimate)
