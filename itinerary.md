# 5-Day Kyoto Trip Itinerary - October 2026
**Budget: Under $1200 for 2 People**
**Dates: October 10-14, 2026**

## Overview
This 5-day Kyoto itinerary focuses on temples, gardens, local food experiences, traditional tea houses, and a day trip to Nara - all while staying under $1200 for two people. October offers beautiful autumn foliage and pleasant weather.

## Daily Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival & Downtown Kyoto Exploration
**Theme: Getting Oriented & Local Food Discovery**

**Morning:**
- Arrive at Kansai International Airport (KIX)
- Take Haruka Express to Kyoto Station (~90 min, ¥2,850/person with JR Pass)
- Check into accommodation: Hotel Sunroute Plaza Kyoto or similar business hotel
- Drop off luggage and freshen up

**Afternoon:**
- Nishiki Market ("Kyoto's Kitchen") - food sampling tour
  - Try: Takoyaki, fresh seafood skewers, pickled vegetables, matcha treats
  - Budget: ¥1,500/person for samples
- Explore Teramachi and Shinkyogoku shopping arcades
- Visit Kyoto Imperial Palace Park (free)

**Evening:**
- Dinner in Pontocho Alley - traditional Kyoto dining
  - Recommendation: Pontocho Kappa Zushi (conveyor belt sushi) or Yakitori Itoh
  - Budget: ¥3,000-4,000/person
- Evening stroll along Kamogawa River
- Optional: Gion Corner for traditional arts show (¥3,150/person)

**Accommodation:** Hotel Sunroute Plaza Kyoto (~¥8,000/night for double room)

### Day 2: Eastern Kyoto Temples & Tea House Experience
**Theme: Historic Temples & Traditional Culture**

**Early Morning (6:00 AM):**
- Fushimi Inari Taisha - sunrise visit to avoid crowds
  - Famous for thousands of vermilion torii gates
  - Hike to summit (2-3 hours round trip)
  - Free admission

**Morning:**
- Breakfast at convenience store or hotel (~¥800/person)
- Kiyomizu-dera Temple
  - Stunning views over Kyoto, especially beautiful in autumn
  - Admission: ¥400/person
  - Explore Otowa Waterfall and Jishu Shrine
- Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka streets - preserved historic districts
  - Perfect for photos and traditional souvenir shopping

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch near Kiyomizu-dera: Try yudofu (tofu hot pot) at Otowa Yudofu or similar
  - Budget: ¥1,200-1,800/person
- Yasaka Shrine exploration
- Maruyama Park (especially beautiful in autumn)

**Late Afternoon/Evening:**
- Traditional tea house experience in Gion
  - Recommendation: Camellia Flower or En Tea House
  - Matcha ceremony with wagashi (traditional sweets): ¥2,000-2,500/person
  - Includes explanation of tea ceremony history and etiquette
- Early dinner in Gion: Try Kyoto-style kaiseki at reasonable price
  - Recommendation: Gion Tanto or similar budget-friendly kaiseki
  - Budget: ¥2,500-3,500/person
- Optional: Evening geisha spotting in Hanami-koji Street

### Day 3: Arashiyama Western Kyoto Gardens
**Theme: Bamboo Forests, Zen Gardens & Scenic Beauty**

**Morning:**
- Breakfast at hotel or local cafe (~¥800/person)
- Take Sagano Line to Saga-Arashiyama Station (~20 min, ¥240/person)
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
  - Best visited early to avoid tour groups
  - Free admission
- Tenryu-ji Temple Garden (UNESCO World Heritage)
  - Stunning landscape garden with mountain views
  - Admission: ¥500/person
  - Includes Sogen Pond strolling garden

**Late Morning:**
- Visit Otagi Nenbutsu-ji (less crowded, unique stone statues)
  - Admission: ¥300/person
- Or explore Gio-ji Temple moss garden (small but beautiful)

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch in Arashiyama: Try soba noodles with mountain vegetables
  - Recommendation: Arashiyama Yoshimura or similar local spot
  - Budget: ¥1,000-1,500/person
- Iwatayama Monkey Park
  - Feed and observe Japanese macaques
  - Admission: ¥600/person
  - Great views over Arashiyama
- Togetsukyo Bridge scenic walk
- Optional: River boat ride on Hozu River (~¥1,000/person)

**Evening:**
- Matcha dessert experience in Arashiyama
  - Recommendation: Nakagawa Seichaho or Itoh Kyuemon
  - Try matcha parfait, warabimochi, or matcha ice cream
  - Budget: ¥800-1,200/person
- Return to central Kyoto (~20 min train)
- Casual dinner near accommodation: Ramen or donburi bowl
  - Budget: ¥800-1,200/person

### Day 4: Day Trip to Nara
**Theme: Ancient Capital, Giant Buddha & Friendly Deer**

**Full Day Trip:**
- Early breakfast at hotel (~¥800/person)
- JR Nara Line from Kyoto to Nara Station (~45 min, covered by JR Pass)
  - Without JR Pass: ¥720/person each way

**Morning in Nara:**
- Todai-ji Temple
  - Houses the world's largest bronze Buddha statue (Daibutsu)
  - Admission: ¥600/person
  - Explore the massive wooden structure and surrounding grounds
- Nara Park
  - Famous for over 1,000 friendly deer
  - Purchase deer crackers (shika senbei): ¥200 for pack of 10
  - Feed and photograph the deer (they'll bow for treats!)
- Kasuga Taisha Shrine
  - Beautiful lantern-lined paths
  - Admission to inner grounds: ¥500/person
  - Free to explore outer grounds

**Afternoon:**
- Lunch in Nara: Try local specialties
  - Recommendation: Kakinoha-zushi (persimmon leaf sushi) or narazuke (Nara pickles)
  - Budget: ¥1,000-1,500/person
- Naramachi district - preserved merchant quarter
  - Traditional machiya houses, craft shops, cafes
  - Visit Koshi-no-Ie residence (free)
  - Nara Craft Museum (¥500/person)

**Alternative Afternoon Options:**
- Horyu-ji Temple (world's oldest wooden buildings) - requires additional travel
- Or explore more of Nara Park and visit smaller temples

**Evening:**
- Return to Kyoto by JR Nara Line (~45 min)
- Light dinner near accommodation or convenience store meal
  - Budget: ¥800-1,200/person
- Early rest for departure day

### Day 5: Golden Pavilion & Philosopher's Path
**Theme: Final Temple Visits & Peaceful Departure**

**Morning:**
- Early breakfast and check out of hotel (store luggage if needed)
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
  - Iconic Zen temple covered in gold leaf
  - Stunning reflection in the pond, especially beautiful in autumn
  - Admission: ¥400/person
  - Allow 60-90 minutes for exploration
- Ryoan-ji Temple (optional, nearby)
  - Famous rock garden (kare-sansui)
  - Admission: ¥500/person
  - Or visit Ninna-ji Temple instead for variety

**Late Morning:**
- Philosopher's Path (Tetsugaku-no-michi)
  - Scenic stone path following a canal
  - Especially beautiful in autumn with colorful foliage
  - Passes several small temples and cafes
  - Free to walk
- Stop at Honen-in Temple along the path (beautiful moss garden)
- Optional: Visit Eikan-do Zenrin-ji for spectacular autumn colors
  - Admission: ¥600/person

**Afternoon:**
- Final lunch in Kyoto: Try Kyoto-style ramen or okonomiyaki
  - Recommendation: Kyoto Ramen Koji (station ramen mall) or similar
  - Budget: ¥1,000-1,500/person
- Last-minute souvenir shopping in Kyoto Station or nearby
  - Recommendations: Matcha snacks, yatsuhashi (cinnamon rice crackers), sake
- Retrieve luggage from hotel storage

**Departure:**
- Haruka Express to Kansai International Airport (~90 min, ¥2,850/person with JR Pass)
- Allow ample time for airport procedures (arrive 2+ hours before international flight)

## Specific Recommendations

### Tea House Experiences
1. **Camellia Flower (Gion)** - Traditional matcha ceremony with English explanation
   - Location: Near Yasaka Shrine
   - Cost: ¥2,200/person
   - Duration: 45 minutes
   - Booking: Walk-in or reserve via hotel concierge

2. **En Tea House (Gion)** - Authentic experience in machiya townhouse
   - Location: Hanami-koji Street
   - Cost: ¥2,500/person includes wagashi
   - Features: English-speaking tea master, kimono option (+¥1,000)

3. **Ippodo Tea Co. (Multiple locations)** - Premium matcha tasting
   - Try their tasting flight: ¥1,500/person
   - Locations: Near Kyoto Station, Teramachi, etc.

### Local Food Spots
**Nishiki Market Must-Tries:**
- Takoyaki at Tamaya
- Fresh oysters at various stalls
- Pickled vegetables at Nishiki Warai
- Matcha soft serve at Itoh Kyuemon
- Tamago (sweet omelet) at Tamagoya

**Pontocho Alley Dinner:**
- Yakitori Itoh - skewered chicken grilled over binchotan
- Kappa Zushi - affordable, quality conveyor belt sushi
- Mizuno - famous for okonomiyaki (though there's often a line)

**Arashiyama Food:**
- Arashiyama Yoshimura - excellent soba noodles
- Nakagawa Seichaho - premium matcha sweets
- Local mom-and-pop shops for yuba (tofu skin) dishes

**Nara Food Specialties:**
- Kakinoha-zushi (persimmon leaf wrapped sushi) at Tanaka or similar
- Narazuke (Nara pickles) - try samples at Nakatanidou
- Mochi-based sweets at Nakatanidou (famous for pounding mochi)

### Accommodation Options

2. **Hotel Granvia Kyoto** - Directly connected to station, slightly higher end
3. **Daiwa Roynet Hotel Kyoto-ekimae** - Modern, good value
4. **Hotel Keihan Kyoto Grande** - Near Shichijo Station, good breakfast

**Hostel Options (if preferred):**

### October 2026 Specific Tips
**Weather:**
- Average high: 22°C (72°F), low: 14°C (57°F)
- Pack: Light layers, light jacket, comfortable walking shoes
- Autumn foliage: Peak typically mid-to-late November, but early colors visible in October
- Rain possibility: Pack compact umbrella

**Events/Festivals:**
- Jidai Matsuri (Festival of Ages): October 22 - after our trip
- Various autumn harvest festivals at temples
- Check local listings for special evening illuminations at temples

**Booking Tips:**
- Book accommodation 3-4 months in advance for best rates
- Consider purchasing JR Pass online before travel for convenience
- Reserve tea house experiences in advance, especially for weekends
- Many temples offer special autumn openings - check specific dates

## Emergency Information
- **Emergency Services:** 110 (police), 119 (fire/ambulance)
- **Tourist Information:** Kyoto Station Tourist Information Center (open 8am-7pm)
- **Wi-Fi:** Available at most convenience stores, stations, and many cafes
- **Convenience Stores:** 7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson - open 24/7, ATMs, food, supplies
- **Pharmacies:** Look for green cross signs, many have English-speaking staff

## Packing Suggestions
**Essentials:**
- Comfortable walking shoes (you'll walk 8-10k steps daily)
- Light layers + jacket for cooler evenings
- Compact umbrella or rain jacket
- Reusable water bottle
- Cash (many small establishments don't take cards)
- Japan-compatible power adapter (Type A/B)
- Portable charger
- Small daypack for daily excursions

**Optional but Recommended:**
- Japanese phrasebook or translation app
- Small gift for hosts if doing homestay/cultural experience
- Binoculars for better temple/detail viewing
- Notebook/journal for travel memories

## Final Notes
This itinerary balances must-see sights with authentic local experiences while maintaining a comfortable pace. The budget includes buffers for unexpected expenses and exchange rate fluctuations. October offers pleasant weather and the beginning of autumn colors, making it an excellent time to visit Kyoto.

**Key Money-Saving Tips:**
1. Convenience store breakfasts save ¥500-800 per person daily
2. Lunch specials (teishoku/set meals) offer best value
3. Many temples have free grounds to explore even if interiors require fee
4. Walking between nearby sites saves transport costs
5. Tea house experiences provide cultural value beyond just the drink

Enjoy your Kyoto adventure!
