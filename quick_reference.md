## TRANSPORTATION QUICK GUIDE
**IC Card (ICOCA):**
- Purchase: ¥2,000 deposit + ¥3,000 initial load
- Where: Kansai Airport, Kyoto Station, convenience stores
- Use: Buses, subways, JR West lines (limited)

**Daily Costs:**
- Kyoto City Bus Pass: ¥700/day (unlimited buses)
- Subway One-Day Pass: ¥600-800
- Typical daily transport: ¥800-1,200

**Airport Transfers:**
- Haruka Express: ¥2,850 each way (KIX ↔ Kyoto)
- Travel time: ~75 minutes
- Luggage: Large suitcases accommodated

## FOOD BUDGET GUIDE
**Per Person Per Day:**
- Breakfast: ¥800 (convenience store: onigiri, yogurt, coffee)
- Lunch: ¥1,200 (ramen, donburi, set meals)

## TEMPLE VISIT TIPS
**Best Times to Visit:**
- Fushimi Inari: 6:00-7:30 AM (sunrise, fewer crowds)
- Kiyomizu-dera: 8:00-9:30 AM (before tour groups)
- Popular temples: Weekdays generally less crowded

**Entrance Fees to Remember:**
- Kiyomizu-dera: ¥400
- Tenryu-ji: ¥500
- Kinkaku-ji: ¥400
- Ryoan-ji: ¥500
- Todai-ji (Nara): ¥600
- Kasuga Taisha: ¥500

**Free Alternatives:**
- Temple grounds often free to explore
- Philosopher's Path: Free scenic walk
- Arashiyama Bamboo Grove: Free
- Nara Park deer viewing: Free (crackers ¥200)

## MONEY-SAVING TIPS
**Food:**
- Convenience store breakfast: Save ¥500-800/person/day
- Lunch set meals (teishoku): Best value ¥800-1,200
- Dinner: Look for lunch specials served until 3-4 PM
- Water: Refill bottles (tap water is safe to drink)

**Transport:**
- Walk between nearby attractions (saves ¥200-500/trip)
- Consider one-day bus pass if using buses heavily
- IC card often cheaper than individual tickets

**Activities:**
- Many temples have free grounds to explore
- Purchase combination tickets when available
- Check for free days/discounted times at museums

## EMERGENCY PHRASES
**Japanese:**
- Help: Tasukete! (たすけて！)
- Where is toilet?: Toilet wa doko desu ka? (トイレはどこですか？)
- I'm lost: Mayoimashita (まよいましました)
- How much?: Ikura desu ka? (いくらですか？)
- Thank you: Arigatou gozaimasu (ありがとうございます)

**Remember:** Most Japanese people are helpful to tourists. Don't hesitate to ask for assistance at hotels, stations, or information centers.
